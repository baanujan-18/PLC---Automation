# Demonstration Video

This video demonstrates the PLC-based occupancy-controlled fan system.

The demonstration includes:

* Entry counting using the S1 push button
* Exit counting using the S2 push button
* Fan 1 operating for 1–4 people
* Fans 1 and 2 operating for 5–8 people
* All three fans operating for 9 or more people
* Automatic fan switching as the occupancy count decreases
* PLC, relay module, power supply, and fan operation
